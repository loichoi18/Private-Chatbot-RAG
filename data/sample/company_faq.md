# Company FAQ — Acme Corp (Sample Document)

## About the Company

**Q: What does Acme Corp do?**
A: Acme Corp is a technology company specializing in enterprise software solutions. Founded in 2015, we provide cloud-based project management, customer relationship management (CRM), and business intelligence tools to mid-size and large enterprises.

**Q: Where is Acme Corp headquartered?**
A: Our headquarters is located in San Francisco, California. We also have offices in Austin (TX), London (UK), and Singapore.

**Q: How many employees does Acme Corp have?**
A: As of 2024, Acme Corp employs approximately 2,500 people across our global offices.

## Products & Services

**Q: What is Acme Project Suite?**
A: Acme Project Suite is our flagship project management platform. It includes task tracking, Gantt charts, resource allocation, time tracking, and integrations with 200+ third-party tools. Pricing starts at $15/user/month for the Basic plan.

**Q: What is Acme CRM?**
A: Acme CRM is a customer relationship management system designed for B2B sales teams. Features include lead scoring, pipeline management, automated email sequences, and AI-powered sales forecasting. Enterprise pricing starts at $45/user/month.

**Q: Do you offer a free trial?**
A: Yes, all our products offer a 14-day free trial with full feature access. No credit card required. You can sign up at https://acmecorp.example.com/trial.

## Technical Support

**Q: What are your support hours?**
A: Our support team is available 24/7 for Enterprise customers. Business plan customers receive support Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM EST. Basic plan customers can access our self-service knowledge base and community forum.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Go to https://acmecorp.example.com/reset-password, enter your registered email address, and follow the instructions in the reset email. The link expires after 24 hours.

**Q: What is your SLA for Enterprise customers?**
A: Our Enterprise SLA guarantees 99.95% uptime, 1-hour response time for critical issues, and 4-hour response time for high-priority issues. Full SLA details are available in your enterprise agreement.

## Billing & Pricing

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), ACH bank transfers, and wire transfers. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice with NET-30 terms.

**Q: Can I switch plans at any time?**
A: Yes, you can upgrade your plan at any time and the new pricing will be prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

**Q: Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?**
A: Yes, registered nonprofit organizations receive a 30% discount on all plans. Contact sales@acmecorp.example.com with your 501(c)(3) documentation.

## Security & Compliance

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Absolutely. We use AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. All data is stored in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers. We undergo annual third-party security audits.

**Q: Are you GDPR compliant?**
A: Yes, Acme Corp is fully GDPR compliant. We offer a Data Processing Agreement (DPA), support data subject access requests, and our EU customer data is stored in our Frankfurt data center.

**Q: Do you support SSO?**
A: Yes, we support SAML 2.0 and OAuth 2.0 SSO for Business and Enterprise plans. Supported identity providers include Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, and OneLogin.

## HR & Internal Policies

**Q: What is the remote work policy?**
A: Acme Corp operates a hybrid-first model. Employees are expected to be in-office 2 days per week (Tuesday and Thursday). Fully remote positions are available for certain roles with manager approval.

**Q: How many vacation days do employees get?**
A: Full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO per year, plus 10 company holidays. PTO increases to 25 days after 3 years of tenure, and 30 days after 5 years.

**Q: What benefits does Acme Corp offer?**
A: Our benefits package includes health/dental/vision insurance (100% premium covered for employees, 75% for dependents), 401(k) with 4% match, annual $2,000 learning budget, home office stipend, and 16 weeks paid parental leave.
